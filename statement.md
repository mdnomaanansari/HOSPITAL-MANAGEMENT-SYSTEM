# HOSPITAL-MANAGEMENT-SYSTEM


## Problem Statement
Managing patient information and hospital workflows manually can be time-consuming and prone toerrors. Traditional methods make it difficult to reliably maintain records, prioritize critical patientsquickly, search for historical information rapidly, and calculate accurate hospital bills without calculation
mistakes.

## Target Users
- Hospitals
- Patients
- Doctors

## SCOPE
To help doctors and hospitals systems workflow easier and fast.
to help patients and workers access easier and transparent.
